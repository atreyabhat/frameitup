Before you post

Instagram supports photo ratios from 1.91:1 to 3:4 and documents uploads up to 1080 pixels wide. Full-resolution files may be resized by Instagram.

Use one canvas ratio for connected slides. Select files in numbered order and enable “Upload at highest quality” in Instagram where available.

The complete photograph is first. Full-resolution overviews can be larger than their slices to preserve detail. PNG preserves rendered pixels; this SDR sRGB editor does not preserve RAW, HDR, or original metadata profiles.
